How IrrigaShield Works

Four simple steps from plan setup to automatic claim settlement.

01
Admin

Create Plans & Slabs

Admins configure crop insurance plans, minimum water thresholds, required shortage days, and payout slabs.

02
Farmer

Register Farms & Apply

Farmers add their farm details, irrigation setup, and crops, then apply for coverage with instant premium calculation.

03
Underwriter

Review & Approve Policy

Underwriters review the farm's irrigation profile to approve policies, with the flexibility to update decisions anytime.

04
Data Provider

Water Report & Payout

Data Providers record actual water availability for active policies. Water shortages automatically trigger paid claims.

Fair Assessment

Simple Underwriting

Every application connects directly to the farmer's registered land, crop, and irrigation setup for fast, accurate policy approval.

Tiered Protection

Slab-Based Payouts

Plans support multiple water-deficit slabs so higher water shortages receive proportionally higher payouts up to the policy limit.

Complete Transparency

Track Every Policy & Claim

Farmers can view their applications, active policy coverage, water shortage reports, and settled claim payouts in one place.
